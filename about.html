About Weathertech

15+ years of engineering excellence in the UAE — delivering world-class MEP solutions with integrity, quality, and precision.

Our Story

Built on Engineering Experience

Weathertech Electromechanical Contracting L.L.C was established with a clear vision: to deliver complete, high-quality MEP solutions to the UAE's growing construction sector. Headquartered in Al Ain, we have grown into one of the region's most respected electromechanical contractors.

From early projects in the Al Ain region to landmark developments in Dubai, Sharjah, and beyond — including work for government entities, military establishments, and major private developers — our track record speaks for itself.

Licensed & Insured

Fully registered in UAE with contractor certifications across all seven emirates.

Multidiscipline

HVAC engineers, electrical specialists, piping teams, and commissioning experts — all in-house.

Est. 2008 • Al Ain Serving All 7 Emirates & Oman

Our Journey

Company Timeline

Est. 2008

Company Founded — Al Ain, UAE

Weathertech established in Al Ain with a focused team of MEP engineers and technicians, serving the Abu Dhabi region.

2010 – 2013

Government & Military Contracts

Secured major HVAC and MEP contracts with the Command of Military Works — establishing our reputation for precision and reliability.

2014 – 2016

Expansion Across Emirates

Expanded to Dubai, Sharjah, and Abu Dhabi — delivering cinema, hospitality, and commercial MEP projects for landmark clients.

2017 – 2019

Large-Scale Commercial Projects

Completed HUB Mall Mirdif, Al Quranic Park Glass House, and IBRI Ayla Hotel — demonstrating capability for complex, high-profile projects.

2020 – Present

Growth & Continued Excellence

Expanding our portfolio, team, and capabilities — delivering MEP excellence for the next generation of UAE infrastructure.
