Complete MEP Capabilities

Whether contracted for a single specialized trade or a turnkey full-MEP package, our engineering teams deliver on schedule and to specification.

01

HVAC Systems

Complete design, supply, installation, and maintenance of heating, ventilation & air conditioning systems for commercial, military, and residential facilities.

  • Chilled Water Plants, AHUs & FCUs
  • VRF / VRV & Package Unit Systems
  • GI / Pre-insulated Ductwork & Ventilation
  • Smoke Extract & Stairwell Pressurization
02

Electrical Works

High-reliability HV/LV power distribution, intelligent lighting, earthing, fire alarm, and BMS integration compliant with UAE utility authorities.

  • MDB, SMDB & Final Distribution Boards
  • Interior, Facade & Emergency Lighting
  • Earthing, Lightning Protection & UPS
  • Fire Alarm, Low Current & BMS Control
03

Mechanical Works

Heavy plant equipment rigging, precision laser alignment, vibration isolation, and commissioning for critical building infrastructure.

  • Water & Air-Cooled Chillers
  • Booster, Transfer & Chilled Water Pumps
  • Cooling Towers, Calorifiers & Boilers
  • Acoustic & Vibration Isolation Mounting
04

Piping Systems

Certified welding, threading, thermal insulation, and hydrostatic pressure testing across chilled water, plumbing, and fire networks.

  • Chilled & Hot Water Distribution Piping
  • Fire Fighting Sprinklers & Hydrants
  • Domestic Water Supply & Drainage
  • Compressed Air & Process Piping
05

Testing & Commissioning

Systematic verification and balancing to ensure every installed MEP asset performs at peak efficiency before client handover.

  • Air & Hydronic Water Balancing (TAB)
  • Hydrostatic & Duct Leakage Testing
  • Insulation Resistance & Earth Testing
  • As-Built Drawings & O&M Handover Packs
06

Maintenance & AMC

Structured Planned Preventive Maintenance (PPM), rapid emergency call-outs, and Annual Maintenance Contracts across all 7 emirates.

  • 24/7 Emergency Breakdown Support
  • Energy Audits & Retrofit Upgrades
  • DLP Defect Liability Period Management

How We Deliver

Project Execution Lifecycle

A disciplined five-stage methodology that eliminates rework, protects schedules, and guarantees handover readiness.

STAGE 01

Design & Shop Drawings

Heat load calculations, clash coordination, and authority-ready CAD/BIM shop drawings.

STAGE 02

Procurement & Submittals

Sourcing certified MEP equipment from Tier-1 manufacturers with full consultant approvals.

STAGE 03

Site Installation

Supervised execution by specialized HVAC, electrical, and piping crews under strict HSE rules.

STAGE 04

Testing & Commissioning

Witnessed pressure tests, air/water balancing, megger checks, and BMS functional verification.

STAGE 05

Handover & AMC

Complete O&M manuals, as-built documentation, DLP support, and ongoing maintenance.
